Editing styles: a page in the popover, or a studio?

Decided 2026-11-06: B, the Style Studio. Someone working on a style doesn't need the canvas, and krita-ai-diffusion edits styles in a window of its own. A stays here for the record.

Sketches only, nothing built. The test tablet's screen at 1120 × 800 dp. The same style, "Moonlit" (Anima with two LoRAs), edited both ways. Agreed already: built-in styles are read-only (Duplicate makes yours), plain words first with the numbers under Advanced, styles are app-wide.

Getting there: hold a style card

In the Generate popover, holding a style card opens a small menu, as holding a brush does. Your own styles: Edit, Duplicate, Delete. Built-in ones: Duplicate only.

A · A page inside the popover: the top

Edit swaps the form for the style's page, as the server button swaps it for the server view. ‹ goes back. Everything is one scrolling column.

A · The same page, Advanced on

The numbers behind "Detail", and the rarer settings. At the bottom, Duplicate and Delete.

B · A full-screen Style Studio (chosen)

Edit opens a screen of its own, as Brush Studio does for brushes. Sections on the left (Model, LoRAs, Prompts, Detail, Advanced, About), the section in the middle, and on the right a preview: "Try it" makes one small picture with the style as it is now.

Side by side

A · Page in the popover

  • Quick: two taps from the cards, and the canvas stays in view.
  • Same place and look as the server view: nothing new to learn.
  • One long column: with several LoRAs and Advanced on, a lot of scrolling.
  • No room for a preview; you try a change by generating on the canvas.
  • Less to build.

B · Style Studio

  • Room: the LoRA list, the server's LoRAs to pick from, and a preview side by side.
  • Feels like Brush Studio, ProCraft's own way of editing a brush.
  • Leaves the canvas: you don't see your artwork while editing.
  • "Try it" needs its own small run (a prompt just for previews).
  • More to build, on both apps.

What a style holds (both options)

Name · model (checkpoint) · LoRAs, each with a strength · prompt template with {prompt} · negative prompt · Detail (Fast · Balanced · Fine), and under Advanced: sampler, scheduler, steps, CFG, rescale CFG, VAE, text encoder, best size, seed. As Krita's style editor, less the settings Anima doesn't use (clip skip, v-prediction, the live-painting sampler).
