The results tray: tuck it away, never lose it

Sketches only, nothing built. Direction A from our brainstorm. The test tablet's screen at 1120 × 800 dp, in ProCraft's colours and sizes. The idea: the results tray (the bar of results at the bottom) is never closed, only tucked away into a small tab, and it comes back with everything still in it. Updated with your answers: the seed moved into ···, and Again is an icon. The desktop works the same with a mouse.

Today, for comparison

The bar covers the bottom of the picture you're looking at, a tap on the canvas paints, and ✕ deletes every result for good.

1 · After a run: the tray is up

Quieter than today: thumbnails, then ↻ (Again), Apply, ··· and ⌄. The seed tag and ✕ are gone: the seed is copied from ···, and ⌄ tucks the tray away. While the tray is up, the canvas doesn't paint: a tap on it tucks the tray away, as a tap outside a popover closes it.

2 · Tucked away: the whole picture, and a tab

The preview stays on the canvas, now uncovered. A small tab at the bottom shows the shown result and how many there are. Tapping it brings the tray back. The tab is there whenever the artwork has results, even after leaving it and coming back, or restarting the app.

3 · Drawing with the tray tucked

With the tray tucked, the canvas is yours again. The first stroke hides the preview, as it does today. The tab stays, so the results are one tap away.

4 · Throwing results away, on purpose

Holding a thumbnail still gives its own Delete. ··· holds "Use the seed" (of the result shown: it goes in the popover, locked) and "Clear all results…", which still asks first. Clearing is no longer the button you press to get the tray out of the way.

5 · Generating with the tray tucked

The tab becomes the progress: a ring and "Generating · 63%". When the first picture is ready, the tray comes up by itself to show it, as it does today.

The rules, in short

  • Up after a run, or when the tab is tapped. While it's up, a tap on the canvas only tucks it (nothing painted).
  • Tucked with ⌄, a tap on the canvas, or Esc on desktop. The preview stays; the first stroke hides it.
  • The tab shows while the artwork has results: the shown one's thumbnail and the count, or the progress while generating.
  • Nothing is lost by tucking. Results go only through a thumbnail's Delete, or "Clear all results…" in ···, which asks first.
  • Hides with other tools' bars (Adjustments, Transform, the Selection toolbar) as today, and comes back after, tray or tab as it was.

Decided so far (2026-11-06)

Its name

The results tray. Its tab reads "Results · 4" (short, so it stays small).

Where the tab sits

Bottom centre, where the tray was.

The seed

Out of the tray, into ···: "Use the seed 18234".

Again

An icon, a round arrow (↻), not a text button. Apply stays the one blue text button.

Still open: whether the Generate popover also lists the results. Suggested: not now, the tab already brings them back.
